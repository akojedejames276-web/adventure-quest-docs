Version 0.2 includes the original procedural 3D Floating Kingdom, themed connections, animated heroes and die, camera controls, synthesized audio, mobile controls and secure guest recovery. Player accounts are not required. Owner acceptance on physical devices and review of procedural art/audio remain pending.

Version 0.4 implements the approved two-dice/final-six finish rule, faces, educational species, less congested layouts, host controls, warned guard shots, lives and an earned-coin life shop. Actual ads and real-money coin packs remain unavailable pending verified provider setup.

Version 0.5 adds an expanded room leaderboard, set totals, 10 points per win and 50 coins per additional 20 points, plus 12 ladders and 10 snakes with long routes.
